1What is privacy and data governance?

Interview answer — say this first
In simple words

Data governance is the set of rules for company and personal data: who may see it, why it is used, how long it is kept, and how it is completely deleted. In a RAG system the same document is copied into many places, and every copy must follow the same rules.

2Why do we need it?

RAG copies data into object storage, a database, two search indexes, caches, logs and traces. A "deleted" document that still answers questions from an old index, or sits inside a trace, is a privacy incident. Laws like GDPR and India's DPDP Act also give people the right to have their data erased.

Without governance

  • Deleted files still appear in answers
  • Personal data sits in logs forever
  • Anyone can see salary or medical documents
  • No record of who did what

With governance

  • Deletion removes every copy
  • Personal data is minimized and masked
  • Access is enforced at search time
  • Every action is audited
Remember — local models are a big privacy advantage

When the models run on your own servers, documents and questions never leave your infrastructure.

3How does it work?

The six principles

1

Minimize

Ingest and log only what is needed. Redact personal data that is not needed for answers.

2

Purpose

Use data only for the purpose it was collected for. Don't train models on it without a legal basis.

3

Access

Role- or attribute-based access, enforced as filters at retrieval time (lesson 23).

4

Retention

Defined lifetimes for documents, chat history, logs and traces, with automatic cleanup.

5

Deletion

Remove every copy, including derived ones like vectors and caches, within a defined time.

6

Audit

Record who uploaded, viewed, changed, approved or deleted what, in a tamper-evident log.

4Example: step by step

Deleting a document completely
  1. Mark the document DELETED in PostgreSQL. Search stops returning it immediately, because of a status filter.
  2. Send outbox delete events: remove its points from Qdrant and its documents from OpenSearch in every index generation still kept, including old ones kept for rollback.
  3. Clear caches that may contain its text or answers based on it.
  4. After the lifecycle checks pass, delete its files and file versions from MinIO.
  5. Purge or redact its text from logs and traces according to policy; let backups expire.
  6. Record the deletion in audit_events.

Handling personal data (PII)

  • Detect PII during ingestion (e.g. Presidio, plus patterns for Aadhaar, PAN or other ID formats) and tag or redact it according to policy.
  • Mask PII in logs and traces by default; give raw access only to a small, audited group.
  • Pseudonymize user IDs in analytics.

5Important terms

PII

Personally identifiable information: names, phone numbers, emails, ID numbers, addresses.

Data minimization

Collect, keep and log only the data that is really needed.

Retention period

How long each type of data is kept before automatic deletion.

Right to erasure

A person's legal right to have their personal data deleted.

Pseudonymization

Replacing identities with codes, so data is not directly linked to a person.

RBAC / ABAC

Role-based / attribute-based access control: who may see what.

Audit trail

A tamper-evident record of who did what and when.

Embedding inversion

Reconstructing text from its vector. The reason vectors are sensitive.

7Advantages and limitations

Good governance gives you

  • Legal compliance and fewer incidents
  • User and customer trust
  • Fast, complete deletion on request
  • Clear lineage of every chunk and answer

Hard parts

  • Many copies of data to track
  • Old index generations and backups
  • PII detection is never perfect
  • Logging less makes debugging harder
Remember — the places people forget

Old index generations kept for rollback, caches, traces, object-storage versions and backups. A deletion is complete only when all of them are handled.

9How to explain it in an interview

Your 1-minute answer

DefinitionData governance is the set of policies and controls that decide who may access data, for what purpose, how long it is kept and how it is deleted, together with an audit trail.

In RAGData is copied into object storage, the database, the vector and keyword indexes, caches, logs and traces, and every copy must follow the same rules. Embeddings are treated as sensitive because text can be partly reconstructed from them.

HowI minimize what is ingested and logged, redact personal data, enforce access at retrieval time, set retention periods, and implement complete deletion: mark deleted, remove from every index generation, clear caches, delete files, handle logs and backups, and audit it.

RegulationThis supports GDPR, India's DPDP Act and the EU AI Act, and running models locally keeps data inside the organization.

Don't say"Embeddings are just numbers, so they're anonymous."
Say instead"Text can be partly reconstructed from embeddings, so we protect them like the original text."
Don't say"We delete the document from the database."
Say instead"Deletion covers the database, both indexes in every generation, caches, files, logs and backups."
